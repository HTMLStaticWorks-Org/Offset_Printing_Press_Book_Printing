Zero-Risk Contract Proofing

How we verify your print job in 4 simple steps

Never sign off on an unknown result. Our comprehensive pre-production proofing protocol ensures your books and print projects look flawless before mass printing begins.

01
Prepress Audit

File Preflight & Swatch Selection

We analyze your high-res PDFs for resolution, font embedding, bleed margins, ink coverage limits, and recommend the optimum paper grades.

02
Contract Proof

Calibrated Epson Color Proof

We output GRACoL-calibrated contract proofs certified with Ugra/Fogra media wedges so you can inspect exact color rendition.

03
Blank Dummy

Physical Dummy & Spine Mockup

For custom books and catalogs, we construct an unprinted physical dummy using your chosen paper stock to verify weight, spine caliper, and hand feel.

04
Express Dispatch

Overnight Courier Delivery

Proofs and dummies are dispatched via tracked express courier right to your desk. Once you approve the sample, plates are imaged immediately.
