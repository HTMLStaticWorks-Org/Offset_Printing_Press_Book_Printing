Helpful answers for print buyers

Frequently Asked Printing Questions

Here are clear answers to common questions regarding prepress proofing, supported file formats, turnaround timelines, paper sample kits, and press-side approval policies.

01 What file formats and color spaces do you require for CTP plate imaging?

We require press-ready PDF/X-1a:2001 or PDF/X-4 files with all fonts embedded or outlined, images at a minimum of 300 DPI at 100% scale, and 3mm (0.125 in) bleed on all outer edges. Artwork should be supplied in CMYK or designated Pantone spot colors (PMS) with an ink density limit (TAC) below 320%.

02 How long does contract proofing and production take?

Digital contract proofs (calibrated Epson proofs with Fogra wedges) are typically dispatched within 24 hours of file receipt. Standard production for commercial brochures and catalogs runs 5 to 7 business days following proof approval. Casebound hardcover books require 10 to 14 business days due to proper glue curing and book pressing stages.

03 Can we attend a press-side pass or inspect press sheets in person?

Yes, absolutely. We welcome publishers, authors, and art directors to attend live press-side approvals in our D50 lighting booth. You will review the first running sheets directly with our head pressman to approve color saturation, density, and ink balance before mass printing commences.

04 Do you provide paper swatch books and physical book dummies?

Yes! We provide complimentary paper sample decks featuring over 40 coated, uncoated, textured, and board substrates. For books and catalogs over 48 pages, we construct custom unprinted paper dummies with your selected stocks so you can verify spine width, hand feel, and weight before plating.

05 What are the advantages of PUR adhesive over traditional EVA hotmelt?

Polyurethane Reactive (PUR) glue offers superior flexibility, tensile pull-strength exceeding 4.5 N/cm, and exceptional resistance to temperature extremes (-40°C to 90°C). Books bound with PUR lay flatter without spine cracking, making it the premier choice for coated papers, heavy grammages, and thick coffee table volumes.

06 What nationwide freight and delivery options are available?

We provide multiple freight tiers including dedicated full-truckload (FTL), less-than-truckload (LTL) with liftgate delivery, and split-shipment warehousing with drop-shipping to multiple distribution hubs or Amazon fulfillment centers. All pallets are shrinkwrapped with moisture-proof wrapping and heavy protective edge corners.
