Our heritage & mission

28+ Years of Industrial Offset Printing, Book Manufacturing & Color Leadership

Apex Offset was founded with a singular commitment: to deliver commercial offset printing and bookbinding with uncompromising precision, razor-sharp registration, and master craftsmanship. Operating from our purpose-built 85,000 sq ft manufacturing plant, we house advanced Heidelberg Speedmaster multi-color presslines, automated computer-to-plate (CTP) thermal imaging labs, and fully synchronized Kolbus bookbinding suites.

Every publication begins with rigorous prepress file preflighting and utilizes eco-friendly vegetable and soy-based inks, certified FSC/SFI sustainable papers, and closed-loop spectrophotometric color control. Whether you require a run of 5,000 hardcover art monographs or 500,000 retail catalogs, our veteran press operators ensure consistent Delta-E color fidelity and structural longevity.

What you can expect

Your complete print production lifecycle, from file preflight to freight delivery

From the moment your high-resolution artwork enters our secure FTP portal to final shrinkwrapped pallet dispatch, our production engineers manage every phase.

1. Automated Preflight & Paper Swatch Selection

We audit your PDF/X-1a files for resolution, bleed margins, font integrity, and ink limits, recommending optimal paper weights and grain direction for your book spine.

2. Calibrated Contract Proofing & Physical Dummy Build

Our prepress lab generates GRACoL-calibrated Epson digital contract proofs and constructs blank unprinted physical paper dummies to verify hand feel, bulk caliper, and weight.

3. High-Speed Press Run & Synchronized Bindery

Heidelberg presslines print signatures at up to 18,000 sheets per hour with closed-loop spectrophotometry, moving directly into automated folding, PUR binding, and packaging.
