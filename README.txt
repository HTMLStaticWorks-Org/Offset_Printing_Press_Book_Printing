APEX OFFSET PRESS & BOOK PRINTING WEBSITE
==================================================
Industrial offset printing facility and high-volume bookbindery commercial website.

Pages & File Structure:
1. Home - index.html
2. Capabilities - capabilities.html (renamed from home-2.html)
3. Services - services.html (renamed from activities-pricing.html)
4. Samples - samples.html (renamed from gallery.html)
5. Quality & Standards - quality-standards.html (renamed from safety.html)
6. About Plant - about.html
7. Contact & Quote - contact.html

BRAND IDENTITY & INDUSTRIAL DESIGN THEME
- Brand Name: APEX OFFSET / Apex Offset Press & Book Printing
- Color Palette:
  * Primary Slate: #111827 (Foundry Charcoal / Machine Slate)
  * Deep Cast Iron: #0b0f17 (Deep Graphite)
  * Process Cyan: #0284c7 (Precision Cyan Accent)
  * Safety Amber: #f59e0b (Process Yellow / Calibration Amber)
  * Crisp Paper White: #ffffff & #f8fafc
- Vector Emblem & Favicon: CMYK 4-color process registration targets, roller cylinders, and open-book binding geometry.

HOW TO USE
- Open index.html in any modern web browser or deploy directly to static web hosting.
- All pages share assets/styles.css and assets/site.js.
- Theme toggle (Dark / Light mode) and RTL / LTR toggle persist via localStorage.
- Dynamic category filter on samples.html (All, Books, Brochures, Calendars, Specialty Finishes).
- Interactive 6-item accordion FAQ on contact.html.
- Automated year attribution via [data-year].
- RFQ / Quote form on contact.html (#quote-form) with client-side feedback validation.
