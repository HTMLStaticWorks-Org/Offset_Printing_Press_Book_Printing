Quality Assurance Protocols

Rigorous multi-stage inspection protecting your brand reputation

From incoming raw paper lot inspection to destructive pull-testing in bindery, our certified quality control team enforces zero-defect manufacturing standards across every volume tier.

01

Continuous Micro-Density Sheet Pulls

Pressmen pull and verify signature sheets every 500 impressions, recording Delta-E readings directly into the production audit log to prevent color drift.

02

Bindery Tensile Pull-Test Certification

03

If any production signature deviates beyond approved contract proof tolerances, we rerun the sheets immediately at zero cost to you.
